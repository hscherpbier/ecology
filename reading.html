Reading list

What I’m reading

Books on ecology, rewilding, restoration and environmentalism, with a short note on each. Years are when I read them.

  • Every Living Thing

    Jason Roberts · 2025

    Linnaeus versus Buffon, and the rivalry over how to name and understand all of life.

    Plants & fungi

  • Entangled Life

    Merlin Sheldrake · 2026

    Fungi, and how they collaborate with plant life and with all life.

    Plants & fungi

  • Nature’s Second Chance

    Steve Apfelbaum · 2026

    Restoring his Wisconsin farm back to prairie.

    Restoration

  • Nature’s Best Hope, and other Tallamy books

    Doug Tallamy · before 2025

    Native plants as the foundation that insects, birds and the rest of the food web depend on.

    RestorationPlants & fungi

  • Forest Walking: Discovering the Trees and Woodlands of America

    Jane Billinghurst & Peter Wohlleben · 2025

    A beginner’s guide to hiking and observing.

    TrailsPlants & fungi

  • Twelve Trees: The Deep Roots of Our Future

    Daniel Lewis · 2025

    Twelve iconic tree species from across the US, and their bigger role and meaning.

    Plants & fungi

  • Half-Earth

    E.O. Wilson · 2024

    How to protect and conserve half of the earth’s nature, especially the most iconic ecosystems. I liked it, though it doesn’t focus much on “regular” nature preservation.

    Rewilding

  • How to Love a Forest

    Ethan Tapper · 2025

    His restoration of a woods on a Vermont mountain, and how restoration is not the same as letting nature do its thing: it takes active intervention.

    Restoration

  • On Trails

    Robert Moor · 2024

    Hiking and the meaning of trails. Along the way he meets the long-distance hiker Nimblewill Nomad.

    Trails

  • Braiding Sweetgrass

    Robin Wall Kimmerer

    Indigenous knowledge, botany and our relationship with plants.

    Plants & fungi

  • Gathering Moss

    Robin Wall Kimmerer

    The natural and cultural history of mosses.

    Plants & fungi

  • Under a White Sky

    Elizabeth Kolbert

    People trying to solve environmental problems that earlier interventions created.

    RestorationWater & fire

  • Wilderness Comes Home: Rewilding the Northeast

    Christopher McGrory Klyza, ed. · 2001

    Scholarly articles on rewilding, with great statistics on wilderness areas in the Northeast, which states do a better job at rewilding, and where the opportunities are for large projects. Quite a bit on reintroducing large grazers and top predators.

    Rewilding

  • Bloemen en haar Vrienden

    Jac. P. Thijsse · Verkade album, 1934

    The richness of butterflies, bees and wasps in the Netherlands in the 1930s, likely much declined since. It mentions a cousin of the Regal Fritillary, the grote parelmoervlinder (Dark Green Fritillary, Argynnis aglaja), already rare then. iNaturalist has recent records from Texel, Vlieland and the Hoge Veluwe.

    Plants & fungi

  • Where the Water Goes

    David Owen

    Managing the Colorado River: the agreements that set each state’s share, and how those rules make it harder to save water and keep it in the river.

    Water & fire

  • When It All Burns

    Jordan Thomas

    Includes an important section on Omer Stewart’s studies of Native American burning across the continent, which created open, forested landscapes for thousands of years. It may help explain how the Regal Fritillary became widespread, and how it held on for some 200 years after the burning stopped.
