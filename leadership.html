Environmental & ecology leadership

Open space, clean water, and connected trails at home

Alongside my research, I work on conservation close to home in Montgomery County, Pennsylvania.

Wissahickon Trails

Board Member · Volunteer hike leader · Trail steward

Wissahickon Trails conserves open landscapes in an otherwise suburban region, protecting land, streams and trails across the Wissahickon Valley.

Wissahickon Clean Water Partnership

Co-chair, Management Committee

A partnership of 13 municipalities working together to restore water quality and manage stormwater runoff in the Wissahickon watershed.

Upper Dublin Township

Township Commissioner

One of my priorities is expanding open space in the township, and expanding trail connections between neighborhoods and parks to create a network of hiking and biking trails.

The Cross County Trail is a major connection for Upper Dublin into the regional Circuit Trails network.
